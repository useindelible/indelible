Indelible is an open-source read-it-later app and knowledge archiver. Articles,
newsletters, PDFs, and EPUBs are captured in full and stored in your library
permanently; videos are saved with their transcript. Links rot; your library doesn't.

## What it does

- **Full-content archiving**: pages are fetched, extracted, and stored at save time.
  The original can disappear; your copy stays readable.
- **A focused reader**: clean typography, highlights, notes, and reading progress
  that syncs across web and mobile.
- **Search that understands you**: full-text and semantic search across everything
  you've ever saved.
- **Mila, your AI assistant**: ask questions across your library, summarize long
  reads, and surface related material.
- **Content in, from anywhere**: browser extension, email-in addresses, RSS feeds,
  and file uploads.
- **Your knowledge tools, connected**: sync to Obsidian and Notion.

## Two ways to run it

- **Self-hosted**: run it on your own hardware with Docker, free forever.
  See [Install with Docker](/docs/self-hosting/install/).
- **Indelible Cloud**: managed hosting, zero setup. Coming soon.
